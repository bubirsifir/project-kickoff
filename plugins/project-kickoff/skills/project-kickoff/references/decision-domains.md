# Decision Domain Map

Use this map to find omissions that could materially change the project. Do not ask every item.

## Purpose and evidence

- Problem, affected user, context, frequency, severity, and current workaround
- Desired outcome and evidence that the problem exists
- Why now, constraints, non-goals, and riskiest assumption

## Users and stakeholders

- Primary and secondary users
- Buyer, administrator, operator, beneficiary, and approver
- Technical ability, accessibility needs, language, geography, and usage environment
- Ownership of content, decisions, support, and operations

## Value and business

- Value proposition and alternatives
- Acquisition, activation, retention, and revenue assumptions
- Pricing, packaging, limits, trials, billing cycles, refunds, and cancellation
- Budget, schedule, team, dependencies, and commercial constraints

## Product scope

- Primary journey and critical moment of value
- MVP, exclusions, later phases, roles, permissions, and edge cases
- Onboarding, empty/loading/error/success states, notifications, search, export, deletion, and recovery
- Acceptance criteria and definition of done

## Content and brand

- Brand personality, voice, vocabulary, prohibited claims, visual direction, assets, and references
- Information architecture, content source, content owner, localization, SEO, and accessibility
- Avoid generic AI copy, invented proof, fake testimonials, fake metrics, and decorative complexity

## Platform and experience

- Product surfaces, devices, responsive behavior, app-store requirements, offline needs, and device capabilities
- Interaction model, accessibility target, design system, component reuse, and realistic data density

## Technical system

- Existing stack and constraints, build-versus-buy, architecture, environments, hosting, and portability
- Data entities, ownership, retention, migration, backup, restore, import/export, and deletion
- Authentication, authorization, tenancy, audit logs, secrets, encryption, rate limits, and abuse controls
- Integrations, API contracts, webhooks, failure handling, idempotency, observability, and rollback

## AI behavior

- Task boundary, knowledge source, freshness, model/tool choices, autonomy, and human approval
- Evaluation cases, correctness threshold, hallucination/failure handling, citations, latency, cost, memory, privacy, and traceability

## Operations and release

- Admin/support capabilities, incident ownership, monitoring, alerts, backups, recovery, and maintenance
- Environments, release phases, feature flags, migration, rollback, stores, domains, analytics, and user communication
- Post-launch metrics, review cadence, experiment ownership, and deprecation plan

## Risk and compliance

- Personal, financial, health, child, biometric, location, or other sensitive data
- Jurisdiction, consent, privacy, retention, accessibility, intellectual property, and sector constraints
- Human review, permissions, auditability, and consequences of failure

For legal, medical, financial, security, or regulatory conclusions, verify current authoritative sources and distinguish product planning from professional advice.
